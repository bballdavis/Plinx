# App Store Metadata

This document holds the copy used when submitting Plinx to App Store Connect.

## App Name

`Plinx`

## Promotional Text

Watch your little ones browse and play with Plex in a safe, kid-friendly interface. Plinx makes parental controls easy and keeps media organized by age.

## Description

Plinx is a child-safe front end for Plex that makes it simple to share videos, music, and photos with your family without exposing mature content.

Designed for kids 2-10, Plinx offers:

- clean, colorful interface with large thumbnails and easy navigation
- built-in parental controls that respect Plex libraries and profiles
- offline playback for car trips and long flights
- seamless Plex integration with an existing Plex account

Whether you're a parent or caregiver, Plinx keeps screen time fun, safe, and under your control.
